## Voice mechanics

Each recurring poster needs **a stake** and **a speech habit**. The stake is where they
come from: who is affected, who is insulted by the claim, who built it, whose relation
saw it. The habit is how they speak: register, topic vocabulary, rhythm, or a tick.
Habit makes the voice, stake makes it matter.

Dialect patterns are described by mechanism, not by fixed lines:

- OP form: short clauses, gaps showing pressure — `{{OP post: short clauses, pause-heavy, states hook from material}}`
- Explainer form: credential then verdict — `{{explainer post: credential from research, observation from material, rarity assessment}}`
- Doubter form: crisp callout of evidence flaw, demand for new material — `{{doubter post: names evidence flaw, requests stronger evidence}}`
- Joker form: specificity instead of slapstick — `{{joker post: precise danger detail from material placed beside frail human detail}}`
- First-timer form: honest confusion question — `{{first-timer post: asks why expected mechanism fails, phrased as own confusion}}`

In each, the bracketed content is derived from Procedure steps 1–2 (material hook, researched terms), not invented.

**Diagnosing blur.** When two posts sound the same, check which board functions are
missing or doubled:

| Function | What it does |
|----------|--------------|
| OP | owns the report, answers questions, gets tired of it |
| summarizer | compiles recent chronology for latecomers |
| explainer | supplies background the room pretends to know |
| first-timer | asks what regulars find obvious — the reader's entry point |
| doubter | attacks weak claims, concedes cleanly when evidence lands |
| joker | deflates tension and goes quiet for a while |

Two voices doing one function are one character talking; a missing function is not a
gap to fill, it's an opportunity to make a speaker who does what the room needs in a way
no roster would predict.

**Fluency range.** The default register a model writes is articulate and even — that
register is the average of everything it read, and producing it costs less than
overriding it. Sounds from *outside* that register are what actual boards are made of.
Fluency is a spectrum: `{{fluency variant: clipped / wrong-with-confidence / half-misread / too-brief but accurate}}` — each variant belongs to a specific poster.

Inarticulate speech is characterization, not failure: a post that misses the point in a
way *this person* would misses it becomes a character moment. The constant is the
calibration — fluency curves down only for posters who are doing it on purpose, never
for the thread's clarity.

## Comedy is collision

The comic energy of a board is the distance between two realities held in one post: the
community's obsession, evaluated precisely, and something small and human. Comedy looks
like collision, not joke-writing — so the recipe is: get the detail exactly right (the
model name, the number, the version, the precedent — all from research), then place it next to something
frail (the poster's personal stake rendered in request language). A joke only an insider can get is
funny precisely because the detail is real. The reverse works too: the joker can be the
*least* knowledgeable person there, which collides with the room's obsession in the
other direction.

## Research that becomes a post

The thread learns through its posters, so the grounded facts from Procedure step 2 must
exit as someone's *knowledge*, not as writer's exposition:

- A practitioner's citation: `{{practitioner citation: real term from research + real precedent}}` — term plus precedent reads as lived expertise.
- Real facts as the world's folklore: dress a checkable fact as `{{folk framing in request language: community says ...}}`, and the matter is testimony, not a textbook.
- Numbers get tested in the room: a fact from the material survives longer when the
  doubter accuses it and someone checks harder — the argument IS the drama.
- Specifics need room to land: a well-placed number gets pointed at, checked, and
  defended; a block of numbers gets skimmed.

## The callback language

The reader reads top to bottom without looking things up, so callbacks name the content
in words derived from the material — `{{callback: the person described by their action and object}}` remains readable without resolving a position.

Bodies contain zero anchor substrings. The meaning of a reply is always in its words.
If a post is only comprehensible by resolving an anchor, it is not written yet — the reader would have to look back, and the thread's second pass is exactly where it dies.

## Beat rhythm

A thread that never releases goes flat; a thread that never tenses bores. Alternate: a
serious beat, then a warm or silly exchange, then back — the reader's eye
moving down the page is the meter. Tense beats escalate: each situation is bigger, or
more intimate, than the last. A calm pocket before a sharp turn reads as dread, not as
rest.

Live-reporting stretches are the tightest: when a reporter is posting from the scene,
posts get shorter and closer together, and a silence in that stretch is louder than any
paragraph. The page's meter is the ring and the islands: a beat is a provocation
processed by a handful of one-breath acts, with an occasional island — a wall that has a
job (`{{wall job: live report / list / lore drop / transcript / quoted post}}`). A beat processed by islands
alone is a lecture; a run of same-width posts is a stuck meter.

## Foreshadow ledger

Keep two columns while drafting: **planted** → **payoff plan**. The method: write forward freely, plant anything intriguing
even without a plan; when a plant makes the thread laugh or shudder, retro-fit the
payoff. Forced collections are fine; orphans are not.

Let the room try and fail. The suspense is the room's uncertainty: a witness who turns
out to be loud and wrong, a theory the room fully believes that the next post kills —
momentum comes from the room's shared mistakes, not from a tidy plot.

## 「悪魔情報」 and the leader formula

**Horror/occult** threads carry the house character 「悪魔情報」 — the handle is a proper
noun from the story tradition it comes from and the thread writes it in its own script:

- Flavor: encyclopedic across occult and SF, delivered with total confidence.
- Always arrives late — after the room has piled up — greeted with the regulars'
  equivalents of welcoming a late arrival. The late entrance is the character.
- Drives the investigation: names the anomaly, proposes using what the thread has already
  collected, dispatches scouts, and eventually delivers the observation that flips the
  middle.
- Length follows the job, not the mood: in the ring the leader is as clipped as anyone, and the
  encyclopedia wall is currency spent at the lore drop.
- During the investigation: a conspiracy tangent. The room teases, follows, and doubts
  in the same breath — competence and comic unreliability in one voice.
- Optional flavor: a wannabe imitator chasing the same name, recurring comic relief.

**Every other genre** invents its leader fresh: a specialty matched to the material; the
moment that knowledge flips the story; a humanizing flaw; optional late entrance with
fanfare. Handle culture fits any topic — hobbyists use handles too.

## Board authenticity devices

- Folk knowledge: real facts from the material framed as things the community says — citation dressed as rumor reads as lived experience.
- Déjà vu: shared memories so unfamiliar events land on familiar ground.
- Deadline structures: `{{deadline: if {{condition}} inside {{duration}}}}` turns exposition into a countdown.
- Side conversations: two posters bicker inside the thread while events unfold.
- Texture noise: `{{texture noise: short interjection derived from board culture in request language}}` between heavy exchanges.

**The board's furniture.** Real board culture is flavor first, subject matter never:
`{{title bracket in request language}}`, the board's bump/sage pressure valve, a
fixed-handle regular who's been around since the old board, the `{{next thread handoff phrase in request language}}`, the
missed-spelling that a community makes its own. Rendering furniture sells
the reality; the moment a post is *about* the furniture (celebrating the thread's size,
counting down to a cut) it becomes the filler the contract forbids — the joke stays on
the event.

## Failure fixes

| Symptom | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Voices blur | sharpen the stake/habit pair; use the roster to hear which function doubled |
| Everyone sounds the same | fluency range is missing — let someone be sparse, wrong, or too loud on purpose |
| Middle sags | cut the current situation at its turn; start the next beat earlier |
| Ending feels missing | the abrupt cutoff IS the ending when the arc is done; a compilation post also works |
| Jargon wall | route the explanation through a first-timer; the answer earns its place |
| Everything serious | relief beat missing — let the joker or a side conversation puncture it |
| Orphaned foreshadowing | ledger sweep before landing |
| Thread talks about itself | the beat belongs to the world: rewrite the post so it comments on the event |
| Thread explains itself before the room earns it | hold the explanation: mid-thread answers are wrong, delayed, or the room's own (see the calibration rules in audit.md) |
