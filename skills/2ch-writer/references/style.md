# Thread Story Style Guide

Companion to SKILL.md. Read before drafting posts. Example lines below are patterns —
render every line in the requester's language.

## Role roster — dialogue patterns

Each named voice pairs one function with one quirk (sentence-final particle,
catchphrase, register clash). Quirks make speakers identifiable without a name tag on
every line.

**OP (reporter)** — plain, slightly panicked register; answers direct questions.

> ok update. it did it again. third time tonight. video after this post

**Summarizer** — numbered recaps; leans on post-number quotes.

> recap for people just arriving: >>12 saw it, >>19 filmed it, >>31 explains why the
> footage matters. short version: we passed "prank" a while ago

**Explainer** — measured tone; cites background before conclusions.

> worked with industrial lasers 6 years. what >>19 filmed matches cavity resonance.
> rare outside labs — that alone rules out kids with pointers

**First-timer** — asks the obvious question; gives lay readers their entry point.

> wait sorry, dumb question — why is everyone acting like "energy came back" is scary?
> isn't that the whole point of the machine?

**Critic** — attacks weak links; concedes cleanly when evidence lands.

> >>44 that photo is from 2019, reverse image search exists. bring something new or
> stop wasting bumps

**Joker** — puns at peak tension, then vanishes for pages.

> lab goes supernova and this guy worries about his thesis. priorities, king

## Pacing beat sheet

Shares relative to total posts (reference: 悪魔情報 serials):

| Beat | Share | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Opening report + reaction burst | ~10% | post 1, immediate mockery / questions / support |
| Situation 1 | ~20% | first escalation; cast reveals functions |
| Tangent pocket | ~10% | off-topic drift, joker peak, false calm |
| Live-reporting scene | ~20% | OP or scout posts from the location near-real-time |
| Mid-game collection | ~20% | foreshadow ledger pays off; leader steers |
| Climax + landing | ~20% | peak event; abrupt cutoff or compilation post |

Jet-coaster rule: puncture every serious beat with at least one silly exchange within
~10 posts (one screen scroll — close enough that readers hit relief before fatigue).
Tension without relief flattens; relief without tension bores.

## Foreshadow ledger

Keep two columns while drafting: planted detail → payoff plan. Author method (城戸,
悪魔情報): write forward freely; plant anything intriguing even without a plan; when a
plant made readers laugh or shiver, retro-fit a payoff. Forced collections are
acceptable; orphaned plants are not.

Orphaned = planted, never collected. Before landing, sweep the ledger: every row pays
off or the plant gets redrafted into pure texture.

## 「悪魔情報」 characterization notes

- Spelling fixed: kanji 「悪魔情報」 in every language's thread.
- Origin flavor: the first case involved demon summoning, hence the handle;
  encyclopedic across occult AND SF, delivered with total confidence.
- Arrives late — the recurring main character who always enters last — announced by
  regulars' cries rendered as equivalents of "took you long enough" / "finally".
- Drives investigation: names the anomaly, proposes strategy ("use the collected fear
  accounts"), dispatches scouts, delivers the decisive observation that flips the
  middle.
- One standing flaw: a conspiracy tangent that scatters the thread mid-investigation.
  Competence and comic unreliability coexist; the crowd teases, follows, and doubts in
  the same breath.
- Optional companion gag: a wannabe imitator chasing the same fame ("the guy trying to
  become 悪魔情報") recurs as comic relief.
- Demographics unstated, like every other anonymous poster.

## Leader formula for non-horror genres

Invent fresh per story: (a) specialty matching the material, (b) the moment their
knowledge flips the story, (c) one humanizing flaw, (d) optional late entrance with
fanfare. Name freely — handle culture fits any topic, hobbyists use handles too.

## Authenticity devices

- Folk-knowledge framing: introduce real facts from the material as things "people
  around here always said" — citation dressed as rumor reads as lived experience.
- Déjà-vu motifs: hook shared memories (old broadcasts, discontinued products,
  childhood warnings) so unfamiliar events land on familiar ground.
- Deadline structures: "if X happens within N days" turns exposition into countdown.
- Side conversations: two posters bicker inside the thread while events unfold.
- Texture noise: occasional single-line posts ("lol", "bump", "watching") between heavy
  exchanges mimic real boards.

## Failure modes and fixes

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Speakers blur together | sharpen quirks; give each voice a signature opener |
| Middle sags | insert the live-reporting scene; move a payoff earlier |
| Ending feels missing | an abrupt cutoff IS an ending when the arc completed; add a compilation post |
| Jargon wall | cap consecutive technical posts at 2; route explanation through first-timer |
| Everything stays serious | apply the jet-coaster rule; promote the joker |
| Orphaned foreshadowing | ledger sweep before landing (see above) |
